# Library of Ruina (`0100E7A019246000`)

Update 1.0.3 (v262144, display version 1.03) over the cartridge base, SDK 15.3.2, Unity 2021.3.19f1 IL2CPP with FMOD on NVN. Modules `main`, `subsdk0` (NintendoSDK_movie) and `sdk`, the `sdk` and `subsdk0` builds of Blasphemous 2.

Status: plays on the Mac at full speed, from a fresh save and from an existing one, through the title menu and into the game; on an earlier, slowed run, also the first Reception to its end and back to the library, with no `[RED]` line. On an iPhone, installed through Xcode, it was reported working with this profile; no log was read from that run. [GitHub issue #739](https://github.com/seedds/nxnative/issues/739) reports the provisional profile on an iPhone 13 Pro Max.

## What it needs

| Setting | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `sys_resource 0x1000000` | [`game.tsv`](game.tsv) | The NPDM declares 16 MB of system resource, enabling virtual address memory |
| `mem_stack 0x28000000` (640 MB) | `game.tsv` | Unity's thread stacks are placed at random in the stack region; see [Memory](#memory) |
| 5 GB guest block, 4 GB alias region (`mem_alias 0x100000000`), `app_pool 0x40000000` | `game.tsv` | The larger stack region comes out of the alias region. The 1 GB pool keeps StandardAllocator's region at 0.94 GB and leaves the rest of the alias region to Unity's own regions, as in the other Unity profiles |
| `EntryScene` order cut (`main+0x21100e0`, `+0x210f5c0`, code over `LoadingManager.TestStart`) | [`patches.py`](patches.py), `main` build `aeea627d…` | The game's own race between its splash and its load; see [Boot](#boot) |
| `shader_groups /Data/=data` | `game.tsv` | The serialized files and Addressables bundles all live under `Data/` |

## Boot

- **Resolved: stuck on the opening movie's last frame; `Data/level1` never opens and a button does nothing** (issue #739's idle game, reproduced on the Mac). `EntryScene.Update` calls `OnPlayEnd` once `SplashScreen.isFinished`; `OnPlayEnd` makes `_option` (`+0x20`) and `_latestData`. A coroutine calls `GotoTitleOrSelectLanguge` when the Addressables load ends, and that hands `_option` to `SaveManager.LoadOptionData`. Nothing orders the two. Measured with `DM_PROBE`, method names from the metadata: stuck, the load ended at frame 496 and the splash at 526, and `StartMoveSceneCoroutine` was never called; working, the splash ended at 491 and the load at 507. The Switch's slower CPU and storage should always put the load last (a run slowed with `taskpolicy -b` and logging, the load at 44 s, works, and 5 of 5 unslowed boots stuck); a Mac finishes first. The race is in the game's code, so a cut changes it: `GotoTitleOrSelectLanguge` calls `OnPlayEnd` first when `_option` is null, and `OnPlayEnd` returns when `_option` exists, so `Update`'s later call does not replace what `LoadOptionData` read. Nothing is delayed. Checked at full speed, no throttling or extra logging: 5 of 5 boots open `level1`; a fresh save reaches the title menu; an existing save plays on.
- **Not** `OnCompleteInitializePlatform_xboxlive` for the cut's code: it has no direct caller but runs at startup on this platform too. A first cut over it started the scene at 3 s and gave the menu of placeholder outlines that the issue reports when a mod forces the movie past.

## Memory

- **Resolved: an `nn::diag` abort at `sdk+0x12ff04` (Result 0).** The backtrace is `nn::os::detail::ThreadManager::CreateThread` (`sdk+0x12a390`) → `ThreadManagerImplByHorizon::MapAliasStack`, under an IL2CPP thread pool worker (`main+0x303f78`): [Thread stacks placed at random in the stack region](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#thread-stacks-placed-at-random-in-the-stack-region). Issue #739's log ends in it at 32 s on an iPhone 13 Pro Max, with 12 `IL2CPP Threadpool worker` threads alive; the Mac at full speed has 2 at 65 s and 85 MB of stacks, so it never fails there. The pool grows when the machine is slow, and `taskpolicy -b` on the Mac gives the same result as the phone: with the provisional 256 MB stack region 3 of 4 boots abort at this address with 116–133 MB of stacks mapped; with 640 MB, 0 of 4, up to 157 MB of stacks mapped and 57 threads created.
- **Current:** through the first Reception on the Mac the alias region reached +3.01 GB of its 4 GB, with at most 1.52 GB mapped, about 2.5 GB resident and 1.1 GB of Metal allocations. With the hardware pool (3.21 GB) StandardAllocator's region alone ends at +3.21 GB and the opening story already reaches +4.00 GB.

## Found on this game

No host or tool change was needed. The boot race is the game's own ([Boot](#boot)). A second case of a general mechanism:

- **Thread stacks placed at random in the stack region:** [the reference entry](../../../docs/reference/memory.md#thread-stacks-placed-at-random-in-the-stack-region); its check as a reproduction on the Mac (`taskpolicy -b`).
- **Combat pages in the hand ignore a first tap in Touchscreen Mode (Touch pointer mode), fixed:** [the reference entry](../../../docs/reference/services.md#a-press-that-lands-with-the-cursor-is-ignored-by-hover-gated-buttons). `BattleDiceCardUI.OnPointerDown` (`0x1FD6420`) calls `OnPdSubmit` (`0x1FD6430`), which asks `GetClickableState` (`0x1FD6520`); that returns 2, not clickable, while `_bEntered` (`+0x133`) is false, and only `OnPointerEnter` (`0x1FD6D00` -> `ShowDetail`) sets it (`0x1FD6EC8`; `HideDetail` clears it). The finger's cursor jump and press shared one frame, and uGUI runs the press before the enter. The touch-ui mod hooks `OnPdSubmit` (gate `icall_resolved`, param 1) and runs `ShowDetail` first when `_bEntered` is false, so the tap lands in one frame with no added delay.
- **Combat renders no v1 bloom (open, fix attempt in the `post-effects` mod):** the scene's three `PostProcessingBehaviour` components are enabled by the engine and disabled again in the same frame by `BattleCamManager.ResetCamSetting`, which calls `Behaviour.set_enabled(false)` on the component it finds on each of five cameras (`_bgCamTransform`, `_fgCamTransform`, `_mainCam`, `_characterCam`, `_effectCam`), so the v1 stack never renders in combat. A first attempt that switched them on again from a v2 `OnPreCull` never ran: no v2 layer renders in combat. The mod now writes 1 into the argument of those calls (gate `combat_keep`). Check the log for `combat: ResetCamSetting camera slot ... kept enabled`, then `v1 behaviour #n first seen at OnPreCull` and `OnRenderImage` lines; `DM_POSTFX_COMBAT_MASK` picks cameras by bit to find which one gives the right bloom.
- **Textures lack the PC's detail, also on High texture quality (open, untested, `max-lod` mod, Graphics group):** the Unity build is 2022.2 or newer (`Texture2D.ignoreMipmapLimit` exists, whose native name is `SetIgnoreMasterTextureLimitAndReload`), so a texture's top mip levels can be cut by the quality level's global mipmap limit, by a mipmap limit group, or by mipmap streaming. The mod hooks `InputManager.Update` (`0x1ED1A84`, the second word: `0x1ED1A80` is Sharp Text's gate), keeps `QualitySettings` global mipmap limit at 0 and mipmap streaming off through icalls, and every 60 to 300 frames sets `ignoreMipmapLimit` on up to 48 `Texture2D` with mipmaps that it finds through `Resources.FindObjectsOfTypeAll` (the `System.Type` comes from `Texture2D.whiteTexture.GetType()`). Check the log for `[mod max-lod] icalls:` (a null pointer means that icall name did not resolve), `mipmap limit N -> 0` and the per-sweep `Texture2D` counts. If the log shows nothing to change and the textures are still soft, the missing detail is in the Switch build's imported texture data and no run-time setting brings it back.

## Performance

- **Current:** on an iPhone (A15, docked, MetalFX, `DM_NVN_GPUPROF` and `DM_NVN_PASSDUMP` on, 1080p RGBA16F scene) the bottleneck is GPU attachment bandwidth, not CPU (`@cpu_ms` about 2 ms). Per frame, from `frames.tsv`: High texture quality with 2x MSAA, GPU 17.0 ms median, 302 MB attachment loads and 496 MB stores; with `DM_NVN_MSAA_CAP=1`, GPU 7.8 ms, 172 MB and 301 MB. Both run about 20 passes a frame, ten of them full 1920x1080 RGBA16F load / store passes.
- **Current:** what still costs with MSAA capped: between the large sprite passes the game switches to a depth-only target (one draw, depth cleared first), which ends the colour pass and makes the next one reload and store the whole scene. In one dumped frame that target (serial 1676) is cleared and stored eight times, 7.9 MB each, and never loaded or sampled; about 63 MB of the 269 MB stored. The default path has no look-ahead to turn those stores into DontCare.
- **Current:** `DM_NVN_TBDR=1` makes it worse, not better: 20 passes a frame become 450 or more (load 12.4 GB and store 7.7 GB a frame by the pass ledger, median frame 30 ms, p90 72 ms, 14 to 20 fps in the worst scenes), and `[gpuprof]` shows first start to last end of 50 to 59 ms against 12 to 15 ms of render time. Cause: `ubd_on()` is false in TBDR mode, so none of the roughly 850 inline uniform updates a frame is deferred (`in-pass uniform updates deferred 0`, `passes broken by copies / buffer updates 142186` by frame 1800), and each ends the pass; without `DM_NVN_TBDR` the same run deferred 135,877 and broke none. `DM_NVN_UB_DEFER=1` keeps the deferral on in TBDR mode.
- **Current:** `DM_NVN_TBDR=1 DM_NVN_UB_DEFER=1` returns the pass count to about 17 a frame (about 240 MB loaded and 150 MB stored by the ledger, 55 to 60 fps docked at 1080p with MetalFX 1.19x and High textures), but the HP and stagger rings drew as rectangles whenever a value updated: the depth-stencil look-ahead stopped at the first inline uniform update and opened passes without the stencil plane the Unity UI masks need ([the reference entry](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#depth-stencil-look-ahead-ended-at-deferred-uniform-updates)). Fix applied, not yet confirmed on the device.
- **Open:** whether `DM_NVN_TBDR=1 DM_NVN_UB_DEFER=1` keeps the picture right in every scene and what it saves: the pass count should return to about 20 and `saved by not storing unwritten attachments` and the store look-ahead should then act on them. Textures that did not load with `DM_NVN_TBDR=1` are not explained by the logs (the same eight `pulling library ... not usable` red lines appear with TBDR off); look for a missing attachment or a red ledger line on a run with the deferral on, and check whether LoR's shaders use bindless handles (`NVN_RB_TEXU`) before trusting the look-ahead's read inference.
- **Open:** with `DM_NVN_TBDR=1 DM_NVN_UB_DEFER=1` the sprite scene still ends its colour pass about eight times a frame (`F3900` dump on an A15 at thermal 2: serials 665 colour and 667 depth-stencil). Each time the game sets a depth-only target (1662, never read), clears it, sets 667 depth-only, clears it, and returns to 665 + 667; `hoist_try` gives up because 667 is attached to the pass, so every cycle stores and reloads the 1920x1080 RGBA16F colour and the depth-stencil, roughly 350 to 400 MB a frame. `DM_NVN_DS_INPASS=1` (`ipc_try` in `nvn_mtl.m`) keeps the pass open: the clear of the pass's own depth-stencil is drawn as a triangle inside it and the clear of 1662 waits as a lazy clear. Not yet run on the device: check `pass`, `rt_load_mb` and `rt_store_mb` in `frames.tsv`, the `[nvn-mtl] in-pass depth-stencil clear` line, and the HP / stagger rings and Unity UI masks.
- **Current:** `DM_NVN_DS_INPASS=1` never acted in the High run (`pass` stayed 17, `rt_load_mb` 239, `rt_store_mb` 150 from frame 1800 to 5100; one `[nvn-mtl] in-pass depth-stencil clear` line, at frame 5204 right after the switch to Low). Each of the seven depth-clear detours a frame comes after a `T src=669 dst=670 ... flags=0x40000000` copy that has already ended the pass, so `ipc_try` finds no open pass; and the sprite passes that are still open are opened depth-only (`use=1/0 mode=4`), which `ipc_try` refuses (`g_enc_mode != 0`).
- **Current:** High against Low, same scenes, same A15 and thermal 2: High has nine whole-texture resolve copies of the 1920x1080 RGBA16F scene (669 to 670, `DM_COPY_RESOLVE`, one per camera) in every dumped frame from 2100 to 5100 and Low has none (frames 5700 onward); with `DM_NVN_MSAA_CAP=1` each is a plain 16 MB read and 16 MB write, about 290 MB a frame, which the attachment ledger does not count, so both qualities show the same passes and load / store totals. 670 is sampled twice in a High frame (the first and last copy's results); the other seven are overwritten first. CPU clock fell from 3.1 GHz to 1.75 GHz at frame 4500 and stayed under 2.4 GHz, with GPU p50 8 ms rising to 17 ms, while `thermal` stayed 2 throughout.
- **Current:** `DM_NVN_RESOLVE_ELIDE=1` skips those dead copies ([the reference entry](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#dead-resolve-copies-after-a-multisample-cap)). Not yet run on the device: check `[nvn-mtl] resolve copy elision` and `t ... resolve copy skipped` in the dump, the number of `T src=669` lines, GPU p50 and `thermal`, and that bloom, refraction-like sprite effects and the scene composite look the same.
- **Current:** with `DM_NVN_RESOLVE_ELIDE=1` on the A15 (docked, MetalFX, High then Low, thermal 2) the scene copies fell from nine a frame to two or three, GPU p50 in High from 7.7 to 8.3 ms to about 7.1 ms unthrottled and from 17 to 18 ms to 9 to 12 ms throttled, and High held 60 fps through the throttle (one 300-frame window with 61 drops); Low is 6 to 8 ms. Passes (17), load (239 MB) and store (150 MB) did not change, and seven zero-draw depth-clear passes a frame (2 to 4 ms) remain. `DM_NVN_DS_INPASS=1` still acted once in the whole run: `ipc_try` now logs its first refusal for each reason (`in-pass depth-stencil clear refused: reason N arg A`, and the first operation not in its allowed list); the reasons are numbered in `ipc_try`.
- **Current:** the first refusal log (`DM_NVN_DS_INPASS=1`, `DM_NVN_RESOLVE_ELIDE=1`) showed few refusals against about seven detours a frame: `copy_t2t` called `rp_flush` before the resolve copy was skipped, and `rp_flush` ends the open pass, so the pass was already closed when the depth-clear detour arrived. The skip now happens before `rp_flush`. The refusals it did log were reason 11 (the pass opened depth-only, `mode=4`) and reason 19 with operation 7 (`OP_UPDATE_UB` between the detour's clears); `OP_UPDATE_UB` is now allowed in the detour while `ubd_on()` is true. Not yet run on the device.
- **Current:** run with the skip before `rp_flush` and `OP_UPDATE_UB` allowed (A15, High): resolve elision active (first case pass 3728), GPU p50 6.6 ms unthrottled (7.1 before) and 60 fps held through the throttle (GPU p50 about 10.7 ms at 2.2 GHz), but `pass` stayed 17 and load / store 239 / 154 MB. `DM_NVN_DS_INPASS` fired once; refusals were reason 11 (the pass opened depth-only, `mode=4`) more than 10,000 times and reason 19 with operation 28 (`OP_BARRIER`). `OP_BARRIER` is now allowed in the detour, `ds_scan` reads across a detour that `ipc_chain` accepts (counting the clears of the pass's own depth-stencil as needing the planes they write), and `ipc_try` and `ipc_fire` accept a pass opened depth-only or stencil-only (only clear-only passes are refused). Not yet run on the device: check `pass`, `rt_load_mb`, `rt_store_mb`, the refusal lines, and the HP / stagger rings and Unity UI masks.
- **Current:** RestoreFX combat bloom, handheld 1280x720 RGBA16F scene, A15 (`[gpuprof]`, 68 sampled frames): each frame has two bloom chains (character and effect cameras), 31 post-processing passes in all, a 640x360 prefilter, seven downsample and six upsample levels down to 5x2, and a 1280x720 uber pass per camera. In the frames where the GPU is not waiting they take 3.3 to 5.5 ms of a 6 to 9 ms frame, about 4.6 ms median: the two 1280x720 uber passes about 0.75 ms each, 640x360 0.75, 320x180 0.74, and the nine levels from 160x90 down about 0.9 ms (20 to 45 us each, mostly per-pass cost). They load 26.9 MB of the frame's 124.7 MB attachment loads, every one refused with `why=22` (see [the reference entry](../../../docs/reference/rendering.md#full-screen-triangles-with-a-constant-z-were-not-proven-to-cover-their-target)).
- **Current:** `raster_facts` now proves a constant-z full-screen triangle for the load elision. Not yet run on the device after a shader rebuild: check that the `why=22` lines are gone, `loads 0` on the post-processing passes in `[gpuprof]`, the attachment load total falling by about 27 MB a frame, and that bloom is unchanged (`DM_NVN_LOADPROBE=1` reports no sentinel pixel). If `why=22` stays, the `Q ... diag` line's `pos_why` names the step that failed.
- **Open:** the small pyramid levels (160x90 down to 5x2 and back, eleven passes a camera) cost about as much in per-pass overhead as in work; one pass or one compute dispatch for them would save about 0.7 ms a frame. A mask of 16 (`DM_POSTFX_COMBAT_MASK`, effect camera only) halves the bloom cost but drops the character camera's bloom.
- **Open:** thermal state 2 (serious) was reached on the capped run at the start; both frame-rate and GPU time need to be read against `thermal` in the `[nvn] on-screen` lines. Capture other scenes with `scripts/perf.py env full` and read them with `scripts/perf.py report` ([CPU time by game function](../../../docs/development.md#cpu-time-by-game-function)).
